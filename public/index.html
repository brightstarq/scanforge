Try the Scanner

Crisp Text Preservation

Grain applies only to bright background pixels — ink stays clean. Letters remain razor sharp at any zoom level.

Channel Misregistration

CCD-bar scanners read R, G, and B in separate passes. Sub-millimetre vibration offsets them, producing the characteristic colour fringing on text edges.

Auto Deskew

Projection-profile algorithm detects and corrects tilt up to ±10°, simulating real-world scanner misalignment convincingly.

Back Bleed / Show-through Advanced

Real paper is semi-translucent. Each page bleeds a faint ghost of its reverse side. Needs 2+ pages to activate.

Focus Gradient Advanced

Depth-of-field blur that increases away from a chosen edge — simulating a document that wasn't fully flat on the glass. Configurable edge and intensity.

Scanner Models

Canon Office, HP Home Budget, worn Library Xerox, Archive Flatbed 2005, Fujitsu Production and Magazine Print — each sets every control to match a real device.

PDF — Structure-Aware Compression

Our compression engine understands the PDF object model — recompresses embedded images while leaving vector paths, fonts, and text streams untouched. Output stays selectable and searchable.

Office files and ZIP

Handles DOCX, XLSX, PPTX by recompressing their embedded images, and repacks ZIPs at maximum deflate.

Quality Controls Per File Type

PDFs: High Quality (150 DPI), Medium (100 DPI bicubic), and Small File (smart rasterization). Images: quality slider from 20–100% with format conversion to JPEG, PNG, or WEBP.

Batch queue

Drop several files at once. Each one is compressed with the settings for its type, and you can download them one by one or all together.
